Hand-authored synthesis of fleet themes, capabilities, and overall agent composition.

> ### 📋 [Executive Summary](VISUAL-STORYTELLING.md)
>
> The goal of this work is to promote human-AI collaboration and prepare people, organizations, and education for an AI-infused future of work. It starts with a doctoral dissertation: the AI Readiness Scale, validated on N=523 with a CFI of .975 and explaining 85.2% of variance in behavioral intention, was built to measure what makes people ready to work with AI and what holds them back. What the data showed is that adoption fails most often where the AI partner has no memory, no calibration, and no proactive judgment. The rest of the portfolio is the program that grew from that finding.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> Twelve repositories carry the proof. AIRS_Data_Analysis is the empirical anchor, the IRB-approved dissertation behind every adoption claim downstream. airs-enterprise ships the validated instrument as a five-minute, twenty-nine-language SaaS assessment. Alex_ACT_ONE is critical thinking as an installable skills pack: sixty skills, fifteen prompts, fifteen always-on instructions, one copy serving Copilot CLI, VS Code, Scout, and the GitHub Copilot app. Alex_ACT_ONE_Muse is the same framework ported to Muse, fifty-two skills triaged against what the host already does natively. Alex_ACT_ONE_Steward governs the lineage: framework authorship, mall curation, fleet visibility, and retirement records. AI-Wars-2026 is a living web-book on the race to define the agentic era, where every claim carries a source ID and corrections publish at equal prominence. Alex_Skill_Mall is the curated marketplace where the battle-tested skills the fleet runs on are published: two hundred hard-won lessons across fifty-plus domains. fabioc-aloha, the page you are reading, is the live evidence: a multi-model pipeline rebuilt every morning and gated on a separate model's review. HeadstartWebsite is an agent-operated trilingual telehealth site for a licensed clinical practice. youtube-mcp-server is a probe into what an MCP server looks like when it is built for agents from the start. Extensions is a monorepo of sixteen standalone VS Code extensions, nine of them on the Marketplace. seo-monitor watches Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools across the portfolio's sites and turns a weekly automated audit into a prioritized fix list.
>
> &nbsp;
>
> The unifying claim is not productivity, it is partnership grounded in evidence: a validated instrument for measuring AI readiness, a teachable discipline for thinking with AI, and a governance pattern that lets both scale across a fleet without losing fidelity. Every project here argues the same point, that the future of work belongs to humans and AI partners that are each held to a standard.
