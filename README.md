## 🔎 Agent Fleet Insights

Hand-authored synthesis of fleet themes, capabilities, and overall agent composition.

> ### 📋 [Executive Summary](VISUAL-STORYTELLING.md)
>
> The goal of this work is to promote human-AI collaboration and prepare people, organizations, and education for an AI-infused future of work. It starts with a doctoral dissertation: the AI Readiness Scale, validated on N=523 with a CFI of .975 and explaining 85.2% of variance in behavioral intention, measures what makes people ready to work with AI and what holds them back. The data showed adoption fails most often where the AI partner has no memory, no calibration, and no proactive judgment. The rest of the portfolio is the program that grew from that finding.
>
> Twelve repositories carry the proof. AIRS_Data_Analysis is the empirical anchor, the IRB-approved dissertation behind every adoption claim downstream. airs-enterprise ships the validated instrument as a five-minute, twenty-nine-language assessment organizations can run. Alex_ACT_ONE is critical thinking as an installable skills pack for Copilot, sixty skills that teach the partner to check its own work. Alex_ACT_ONE_Muse ports the same discipline to Muse, fifty-two skills triaged against what the host does natively. Alex_Skill_Mall publishes the fleet's proven lessons as a curated marketplace. AI-Wars-2026 is a living web-book on the race to define the agentic era, every claim sourced, every correction public. harness-assessment tests AI tools from the operator's seat against the Harness Canvas and publishes its verdicts with their traces. spotify-skill is a production Spotify integration packaged as a skill, and the reference for what a finished skill looks like. SurveyFunnel turns survey response work into a playable training game, live on Azure. circuit-breaker is the creative range proof, a cyberpunk arcade remix built by the same partnership. HeadstartWebsite is a trilingual telehealth site for a licensed clinical practice, operated with an AI partner. fabioc-aloha, the page you are reading, rebuilds itself every morning on a deterministic pipeline: topics in, portfolio out, no model in the loop.
>
> The unifying claim: partnership grounded in evidence. A validated instrument for measuring readiness, a teachable discipline for thinking with AI, and working systems that hold both to a standard. The future of work belongs to humans and AI partners that are each held to one.
